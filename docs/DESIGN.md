Industrial dark studio. Not cyberpunk neon terminal. Not purple SaaS.

| Token | Role |
|---|---|
| Ground | near-black warm ink `#07080b` |
| Ink | warm white `#eceae4` |
| Mute | `#9a968c` |

| Warn | warm amber `#d4a24c` — rare |

## Type
- Display / UI: Hanken Grotesk (not Space Grotesk, not Inter)
- Prose: Newsreader
- Data only: IBM Plex Mono

## Banned (slop law)
Pill kickers with pulse dots · glowy full-round CTAs · gradient headline fills · purple/cyan orb soup · grid paper · mono as house voice · kitchen-sink cards · opacity-0 entrance traps · hover-lift on everything

## Signature artifacts
1. **Money machine** diagram (power / hardware / compute → AI → $)
2. **Live equity curve** (real Dhan data)

## Buttons
6–8px radius, solid fill, no blur underglow. Primary = accent on dark ink label. Secondary = hairline border.

## Motion
Transform/opacity only · short · content always visible without JS.
